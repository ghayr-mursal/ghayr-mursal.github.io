English translation ＋

The funny one

Before the majlis I sit in the car a while. I pick a story that needs no explaining and check that my voice still knows how to laugh. Inside, they say you brighten the room. I laugh, pour coffee, listen. Just once I want to walk in somewhere without it being my job to make anyone feel better.
